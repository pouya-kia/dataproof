# Roadmap and gate boundaries

Phase 0: semantics, related work, research question and baseline feasibility. Phase 1: this limited NoUndo pair-world CLI and independently replayed witness. Phase 2 (authorized 2026-10-18): replay/error hardening, broader property/mutation/differential tests and reproducible tiny-reference benchmark evidence. Phase 2 does not expand the accepted SQL grammar. Later NoUndo SQL expansion, Doppelgänger, Proof2Test, TimeProof, ChangeProof, RepairProof, ResourceProof and provider adapters are research plans only. No placeholder implementation is exposed for them.

Before claiming a scoped theorem, we need a written proof and checked premises. Before claiming a research contribution, we need fair runnable competitor obligations, matched benchmark methods and reviewed negative results. Papers 1–4 and software/demo submissions remain `NOT_READY` until their individual evidence gates pass. A working demo is useful on its own, independent of novelty or publication.
